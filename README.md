# Trapped

A 1st-person Horror game. Escape the winter by finding codes randomly found all over the map, whilst trying to evade a monster lurking and hunting for you. 

## How it was made

- This game was made in unity in the language of C#.
- A LOT of youtube videos and mental health.

## Notes

- If you're unlucky, you might encounter a sound issue bug when you evade the monster (he stops chasing you), and you might crash. I tried fixing it trust but the timeframe was too tight, I didn't have enough time.
- With that in mind, try to win the game with the monster chasing you the entire time (if it spots you). Ive done it, trust, its possible. 
- I'm more than happy to provide the documentation (for my grades :( ) that went with making this game.
- And also keep your expectations quite low and  you could possibly enjoy it :)
